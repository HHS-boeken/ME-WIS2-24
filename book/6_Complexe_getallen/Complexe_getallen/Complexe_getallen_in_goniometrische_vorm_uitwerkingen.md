# 7.3.1 Uitwerkingen

## Opgave 7.3.1a

::::{admonition} Antwoord
:class: important, dropdown

$$
z = \sqrt{2} \left( \cos \left( \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right)  + i \sin \left( \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right) \right)
$$

:::{dropdown} Uitwerking

Schrijf in goniometrische vorm

$$
z = 1 + i
$$

De modulus is:

$$
\begin{align*}
r = |z| &= \sqrt{ (1)^2 + (1)^2}  \\
&= \sqrt{ 1 + 1  } \\
&= \sqrt{2}
\end{align*}
$$

Het argument is:

$$
\begin{align*}
\theta = arg(z) &=  \tan^{-1} \left( \dfrac{1}{ 1 } \right) \\
&=  \tan^{-1} (1)\\
&= \dfrac{\pi}{4}
\end{align*}
$$

Schrijf in goniometrische vorm:

$$
\begin{align*}
z &= r(\cos \theta + i \sin \theta)\\
z &= \sqrt{2} \left( \cos \left( \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right)  + i \sin \left( \dfrac{\pi}{4} \right) \right)
\end{align*}
$$
:::
::::
