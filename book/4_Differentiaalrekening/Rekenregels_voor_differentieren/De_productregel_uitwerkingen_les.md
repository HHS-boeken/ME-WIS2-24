## **Productregel zonder kettingregel**  
De **productregel** luidt:  
\[
(f \cdot g)' = f' g + f g'
\]
waarbij \( f(x) \) en \( g(x) \) functies zijn.

### **Opgaven zonder kettingregel:**
1. \( f(x) = e^x \cdot \sin x \)
2. \( f(x) = \ln x \cdot x^2 \)
3. \( f(x) = \cos x \cdot e^x \)
4. \( f(x) = (x+3) \cdot \sqrt{x} \)
5. \( f(x) = (x^2 - 1) \cdot \tan x \)
6. \( f(x) = 2^x \cdot \log_3(x) \)
7. \( f(x) = 5^x \cdot \log_4(x^2 + 1) \)
8. \( f(x) = 3^x \cdot \log_2(x+2) \)
9. \( f(x) = 7^x \cdot \log_5(x^3 - 1) \)
10. \( f(x) = 4^x \cdot \log_6(x-1) \)

### **Antwoorden zonder kettingregel:**
1. \( f'(x) = e^x (\sin x + \cos x) \)
2. \( f'(x) = 2x \ln x + x \)
3. \( f'(x) = e^x (\cos x - \sin x) \)
4. \( f'(x) = \sqrt{x} + \frac{x+3}{2\sqrt{x}} \)
5. \( f'(x) = 2x \tan x + (x^2 - 1) \sec^2 x \)
6. \( f'(x) = (2^x \cdot \ln(2)) \cdot \log_3(x) + 2^x \cdot \frac{1}{x \ln(3)} \)
7. \( f'(x) = (5^x \ln(5)) \cdot \log_4(x^2 + 1) + 5^x \cdot \frac{2x}{(x^2+1) \ln(4)} \)
8. \( f'(x) = (3^x \ln(3)) \cdot \log_2(x+2) + 3^x \cdot \frac{1}{(x+2) \ln(2)} \)
9. \( f'(x) = (7^x \ln(7)) \cdot \log_5(x^3 - 1) + 7^x \cdot \frac{3x^2}{(x^3 - 1) \ln(5)} \)
10. \( f'(x) = (4^x \ln(4)) \cdot \log_6(x-1) + 4^x \cdot \frac{1}{(x-1) \ln(6)} \)
